# Claudex 1.3.16

Development build of 2026-10-10; not yet a published release. App **1.3.16**
bundles Claude Mod **0.8.18**. These notes summarize changes since 1.3.15.

## `/claudex:warm on` warms for four hours by default

- Without a limit the command used 60 minutes and 3 requests. At a 1h TTL the
  first request is due 55 minutes after a reply, so at most one ever fitted.
- It now behaves exactly as `for=4h` on Claude and on Codex: up to 4 requests
  at a 1h TTL, 60 at a 5m TTL and 9 on Codex. `rounds=`, `for=` and `until=`
  still choose something else.
- A five-minute TTL now uses up to 60 requests where it used 3; give a limit
  when that is not wanted.
- The advanced `/claudex warm on` and the CLI keep 60 minutes and 3 requests.

## The default can be changed in the Mod pane

- The Cache settings tab has a **Default warming limit** choice, from 1 to 168
  hours or 3 to 100 requests, previewed and confirmed like the other settings.
- `/claudex warm limit for=6h` saves any `rounds=`, `for=` or `until=HH:MM`
  value after its `confirm` command. A day of the month is not accepted as a
  default.
- The saved limit applies only when `/claudex:warm on` is given none. It
  changes neither the TTL nor a warm-up already running, and it is Claude-only:
  Codex keeps four hours.

Claude Mod 0.8.18 carries the new default and the setting. Checked with
injected data, the native validator and the native test kit.
