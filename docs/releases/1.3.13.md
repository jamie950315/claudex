# Claudex 1.3.13

Development build of 2026-10-10; not yet a published release. App **1.3.13**
bundles Claude Mod **0.8.16**. These notes summarize changes since 1.3.12.

## Choose how many times, how long or until when to warm

- `/claudex:warm on` accepts your own limits on Claude and on Codex:
  `rounds=N` (1-100 warm requests), `for=90m|3h|1h30m` (up to 24 hours) or
  `until=HH:MM` (the next occurrence of that local 24-hour time). `for` and
  `until` exclude each other; either combines with `rounds`, and the first limit
  reached stops warming.
- A time limit alone allows as many requests as fit in it; `rounds` alone may
  take up to 24 hours. A time no request fits in, or one that would need more
  than 100 requests, is refused with the reason. Without these arguments the
  defaults remain 60 minutes and 3 requests.
- The warming summary has a last line with the requests used, the limit and the
  end time, in all nine languages.
- The limits use the existing bounded policy fields; identity, idle, budget and
  no-replay checks are unchanged. More requests use more plan quota.

Claude Mod 0.8.16 carries the command and summary changes. A session that
already loaded 0.8.15 keeps it until a new session or reload.

Checked with injected data. Real refreshes under the new limits are a separate
acceptance.
