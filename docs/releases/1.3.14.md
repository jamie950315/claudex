# Claudex 1.3.14

Development build of 2026-10-10; not yet a published release. App **1.3.14**
bundles Claude Mod **0.8.17**. These notes summarize changes since 1.3.13.

## One warming limit, with higher maximums

- `/claudex:warm on` now takes exactly one of `rounds=`, `for=` or `until=`.
  A combination is refused; 1.3.13 allowed `rounds` with a time limit.
- `rounds` goes up to 500 (was 100) and `for` up to 168 hours (was 24).
- `until` also accepts a day of the month, `until=11:18:30` for 18:30 on the
  next 11th. An end more than 168 hours away is refused.
- `rounds` must fit in 168 hours at the refresh interval, and a time limit may
  not need more than 500 requests; both are refused with the exact maximum.
- The advanced `maxMinutes` and `maxRefreshes` bounds follow: up to 10080 and 500.

## The attempt ledger no longer fills up for good

- Every warm request leaves a record, and the ledger holds 2048 of them. They
  were never removed, so enough long runs would have ended warming permanently.
- Enabling warming now drops that conversation's settled records from earlier
  enrollments. Once the ledger is half full it also drops those of other
  conversations whose warming is stopped and past its end time; their status
  then shows no totals. Uncertain and unfinished records are always kept, and a
  ledger that is still full refuses new requests as before.

Claude Mod 0.8.17 carries the command changes. Checked with injected data.
