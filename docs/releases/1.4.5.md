# Claudex 1.4.5

Prepared on 2026-10-13. App **1.4.5** bundles Claude Mod **0.8.24**, unchanged.
App and Mod versions are separate. These notes summarize changes since the
last published release, 1.4.4.

## A finished Codex sub-agent no longer pauses a conversation

- When a sub-agent finishes, Codex records the notification on the turn that
  started the agent, even when that turn has already closed, and reports it
  inside that turn. Claudex places such late arrivals where they actually
  arrived, but it could prove that only for commands, so the conversation
  paused with "late arrival has an ambiguous identity, active turn or
  completion order".
- The same pause appeared earlier still: when a late command or notification
  belonged to a turn that had failed (for example "model is at capacity")
  after the last reply. That turn is not part of the synchronized history
  yet, but the check looked for it there and refused.
- A late sub-agent notification is now proven and placed like a late command,
  and a late item of a turn that is not synchronized yet is left until that
  turn is. Late items of any other type, a turn missing from inside the
  synchronized range, and a real change to earlier history still pause.
  Nothing is rebased or rewritten.

Found in one real conversation. Checked over 45 local Codex histories that
contain late commands or sub-agent notifications: 20 were proven before and
are proven identically now, 2 that were refused are proven now, and none
that was proven is refused. Also covered by synthetic tests.
