# Claudex 2.0.17

Released on 2026-10-23. App **2.0.17** bundles Claude Mod **0.8.75**.
[Release and downloads](https://github.com/jamie950315/claudex/releases/tag/v2.0.17).

## Removed temporary folders no longer strand unpublished handoffs

- Check saved working directories inside the synchronization coordinator as well
  as the watcher, including removal during a native read or snapshot preparation.
- If a prepared new Codex snapshot was never written, normal recovery independently
  verifies rollout and native identity absence, revokes archive actions, and
  atomically cancels the preparation and stops tracking that conversation.
  Other conversations can continue, including after a restart.
- Handle Codex's `thread not loaded` response only with independent loaded,
  stored, archived, rollout and ledger absence proofs. The error alone does not
  authorize cancellation. Applied, reused, promoted and uncertain handoffs keep
  their existing recovery guards; originals and checkpoints remain intact.

## Install or update

Download `Claudex-v2.0.17-macOS-arm64.zip`, unzip it and place **Claudex.app**
in **Applications**. For an update, quit Claudex and wait for its normal drain
before replacing the app. Reopen it to
update its bundled Mod and recover through the normal watcher. Do not resend
messages or edit native histories to clear this warning. Existing Claude sessions
can retain their previous Mod until their next normal load.

Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later. This build has an **Apple Development signature
and is not notarized**. If macOS blocks the first launch, choose **Open Anyway**
in **System Settings › Privacy & Security**. The ZIP and `SHA256SUMS` are available
on the release page.
