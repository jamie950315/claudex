# Claudex 1.3.19

Development build of 2026-10-10; not yet a published release. App **1.3.19**
bundles Claude Mod **0.8.21**. These notes summarize changes since 1.3.18.

## Choose what your own message does to a running warm-up

- The Cache settings window has **When you send a message**, for Claude Code
  and Codex.
- **Keep warming and restart the timer** is the default and the earlier
  behavior: the next warm request is timed from the reply to your message, and
  the chosen limit (requests, duration or end time) stays as it was.
- **Stop warming** ends the schedule with that message. Status then shows
  "Stopped by your message"; turn warming on again when wanted.
- The choice applies to warm-ups already running. The first message after
  turning warming on in a conversation with no reply yet only gives warming
  its starting point and never stops it. Commands that make no model request,
  such as `/claudex:warm status`, do not count as a message.

Checked with injected data, the native validator and the native test kit.
