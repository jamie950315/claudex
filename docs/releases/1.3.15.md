# Claudex 1.3.15

Development build of 2026-10-10; not yet a published release. App **1.3.15**
bundles Claude Mod **0.8.17**. The bundled Mod payload is unchanged. These notes
summarize changes since 1.3.14.

## Warming no longer stops after 64 conversations

- Each provider remembers at most 64 conversations that ever enabled warming
  and 2048 enable/disable receipts. Neither was ever removed, so warming would
  eventually have refused every new conversation.
- When the conversations, the receipts or the attempt records are half used,
  enabling warming retires the other conversations whose warming is stopped
  and past its end time, together with their receipts and settled records.
- A conversation with an uncertain or unfinished attempt is kept as evidence.
  Running enrollments and stopped ones still inside their time limit are never
  retired. Retiring changes nothing in Claude or Codex: it only forgets
  finished bookkeeping, and such a conversation can enable warming again.

Checked with injected data.
