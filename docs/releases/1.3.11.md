# Claudex 1.3.11

Development build of 2026-10-10; not yet a published release. App **1.3.11**
bundles Claude Mod **0.8.15**. The bundled Mod payload is unchanged. These notes
summarize changes since 1.3.10.

## A blocked command on Codex 0.162 no longer pauses its conversation

- A prompt that a hook blocks, such as `/claudex:warm on`, leaves an empty turn
  that Claudex proves from the rollout before leaving it out of the dialogue.
  Codex 0.162 writes two more fields there: `turn_attribution` on the start and
  `root_turn_id` on the completion. The proof refused them with "empty turn
  start differs from the API" and the conversation stayed paused.
- Both are accepted in their exact observed form: the attribution names the
  same turn and root, its trigger is `composer`, and it has no parent turn or
  initiating agent. Any other value or field is still refused.

Checked with synthetic rollouts and a read-only export of the affected
conversation on this Mac.
